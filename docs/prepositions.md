# Instrumental Prepositions & Verbs

The Instrumental is used with specific prepositions for accompaniment and location, plus certain verbs and constructions.

---

## Instrumental Prepositions

These prepositions **always** take the instrumental case:

| Preposition | Meaning | Example |
|:------------|:--------|:--------|
| **s/se** | with | Jdu s bratrem. |
| **před** | in front of, before | Stojím před domem. |
| **za** | behind, beyond | Auto je za garáží. |
| **nad** | above, over | Lampa visí nad stolem. |
| **pod** | under, below | Kočka je pod postelí. |
| **mezi** | between, among | Sedím mezi bratrem a sestrou. |

---

## S/Se + Instrumental (With)

The most common instrumental preposition! Use **s** before consonants, **se** before difficult clusters (s, z, š, ž, or consonant clusters).

### Accompaniment

| Czech | English |
|:------|:--------|
| Jdu **s kamarádem**. | I go with a friend. |
| Mluvím **se sestrou**. | I talk with my sister. |
| Bydlím **s rodiči**. | I live with my parents. |
| Jdu **s ní** do kina. | I'm going to the cinema with her. |
| Pracuji **s kolegou**. | I work with a colleague. |

### Food/Drink

| Czech | English |
|:------|:--------|
| Káva **s mlékem**. | Coffee with milk. |
| Čaj **s citrónem**. | Tea with lemon. |
| Chleba **s máslem**. | Bread with butter. |
| Pizza **se sýrem**. | Pizza with cheese. |

### Expressions

| Czech | English |
|:------|:--------|
| S radostí! | With pleasure! |
| S úsměvem. | With a smile. |
| S chutí! | Bon appétit! (With appetite!) |
| Co je s tebou? | What's wrong with you? |

:::tip S vs Se
- **s** = before most consonants (s bratrem, s matkou)
- **se** = before s, z, š, ž, or consonant clusters (se sestrou, se psem, se mnou)
:::

## Před + Instrumental (In Front Of, Before)

### Physical Location

| Czech | English |
|:------|:--------|
| Stojím **před domem**. | I'm standing in front of the house. |
| Čekám **před školou**. | I'm waiting in front of school. |
| Auto je **před garáží**. | The car is in front of the garage. |
| Strom je **před oknem**. | The tree is in front of the window. |

### Time (Before)

| Czech | English |
|:------|:--------|
| **Před rokem**. | A year ago. |
| **Před týdnem**. | A week ago. |
| **Před válkou**. | Before the war. |
| **Před obědem**. | Before lunch. |

---

## Za + Instrumental (Behind, Beyond)

### Physical Location

| Czech | English |
|:------|:--------|
| Auto je **za domem**. | The car is behind the house. |
| Zahrada je **za plotem**. | The garden is behind the fence. |
| Co je **za tou horou**? | What's beyond that mountain? |
| Sedí **za stolem**. | He sits behind the table. |

:::note Za + Motion = Accusative!
When expressing motion (going behind), use accusative:
- Jdu **za dům**. (I'm going behind the house.) - accusative
- Stojím **za domem**. (I'm standing behind the house.) - instrumental
:::

## Mezi + Instrumental (Between, Among)

| Czech | English |
|:------|:--------|

## Means & Instruments (No Preposition!)

For expressing HOW you do something, use instrumental **without** a preposition:

| Czech | English |
|:------|:--------|
| Píšu **perem**. | I write with a pen. |
| Jedu **autem**. | I travel by car. |
| Krájím **nožem**. | I cut with a knife. |
| Jím **vidličkou**. | I eat with a fork. |
| Platím **kartou**. | I pay by card. |
| Mluvím **česky**. | I speak Czech. |

### Transportation

| Czech | English |
|:------|:--------|
| Jedu **autem**. | I go by car. |
| Jedu **autobusem**. | I go by bus. |
| Jedu **vlakem**. | I go by train. |
| Jedu **metrem**. | I go by metro. |
| Letím **letadlem**. | I fly by plane. |
| Jdu **pěšky**. | I go on foot. |

## Identity Verbs (To Be/Become)

With **být** (to be) and **stát se** (to become), use instrumental for professions and roles:

| Czech | English |
|:------|:--------|
| Jsem **studentem**. | I am a student. |
| Ona je **učitelkou**. | She is a teacher. |
| Stal se **lékařem**. | He became a doctor. |
| Chci být **inženýrem**. | I want to be an engineer. |

### Verbs That Take Instrumental

| Verb | Meaning | Example |
|:-----|:--------|:--------|
| **být** | to be | Jsem studentem. |
| **stát se** | to become | Stal se prezidentem. |
| **zůstat** | to remain | Zůstal přítelem. |
| **zabývat se** | to deal with | Zabývám se prací. |
| **zajímat se** | to be interested in | Zajímám se o historii. |

---

## Question Words

| Czech | English | Example |
|:------|:--------|:--------|
| **Kým?** | By whom? / With whom? | S kým jdeš? |
| **Čím?** | By what? / With what? | Čím píšeš? |
| **Před čím?** | In front of what? | Před čím stojíš? |
| **Za kým?** | Behind whom? | Za kým sedíš? |

## Common Mistakes

:::warning Watch Out!

**❌ Jdu s bratr** (nominative!)
**✅ Jdu s bratrem** (instrumental)

---

**❌ Píšu s perem** (unnecessary preposition!)
**✅ Píšu perem** (means = no preposition)

---

**❌ Stojím před domě** (locative ending!)
**✅ Stojím před domem** (instrumental)

---

**❌ Jsem student** (nominative predicate)
**✅ Jsem studentem** (instrumental with být)
:::
