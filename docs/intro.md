# The Instrumental Case (7. pád - Instrumentál)

Welcome to the complete guide to the Czech Instrumental case! This is the **"with/by" case** - use it for tools, means, and accompaniment.

## Why Master the Instrumental?

The Instrumental is **essential** for expressing:

1. **Means & instruments** - "I write **with a pen**"
2. **Accompaniment** - "I go **with my friend**"
3. **Location** - "behind the house," "under the table"
4. **Identity** - "I am **a teacher**"

Master the Instrumental, and you can express how you do things and who you're with!

## Quick Facts

| Question | Answer |
|:---------|:-------|
| **Czech name** | Instrumentál (7. pád) |
| **Questions** | Kým? Čím? (By whom? By what?) |
| **Key feature** | Used for means, tools, accompaniment |
| **Main uses** | With, by, behind, under, between |
| **Difficulty** | ⭐⭐ (Medium - regular endings) |

---

## The Core Concept

:::tip Think: HOW? WITH WHAT? WITH WHOM?
The Instrumental answers **"By what means?"** or **"With whom?"**:

- Píšu **perem**. (I write **with a pen**.)
- Jedu **autem**. (I travel **by car**.)
- Jdu **s bratrem**. (I go **with my brother**.)
- Stojím **před domem**. (I stand **in front of the house**.)
:::

---

## Instrumental vs Other Cases

:::info Key Distinctions
| Meaning | Case | Example |
|:--------|:-----|:--------|
| **Direct object** | Accusative | Vidím **bratra**. (I see my brother.) |
| **Indirect object** | Dative | Dám to **bratrovi**. (I give it to my brother.) |
| **With/by** | Instrumental | Jdu **s bratrem**. (I go with my brother.) |
:::

## The Key Patterns

| Gender | Singular | Plural | Example |
|:-------|:---------|:-------|:--------|
| Masculine | -em | -y/-i | studentem → studenty |
| Feminine -a | -ou | -ami | ženou → ženami |
| Feminine soft | -í | -emi | růží → růžemi |
| Neuter -o | -em | -y | městem → městy |
| Neuter -e | -em | -i | mořem → moři |

## Main Uses of Instrumental

### 1. Means & Instruments (no preposition)
| Czech | English |
|:------|:--------|
| Píšu **perem**. | I write with a pen. |
| Jedu **autem**. | I travel by car. |
| Krájím **nožem**. | I cut with a knife. |

### 2. Accompaniment (with "s/se")
| Czech | English |
|:------|:--------|
| Jdu **s kamarádem**. | I go with a friend. |
| Mluvím **se sestrou**. | I talk with my sister. |

### 3. Location Prepositions
| Czech | English |
|:------|:--------|
| Stojím **před domem**. | I stand in front of the house. |
| Auto je **za garáží**. | The car is behind the garage. |
| Lampa visí **nad stolem**. | The lamp hangs above the table. |

### 4. Identity (with "být")
| Czech | English |
|:------|:--------|
| Jsem **učitelem**. | I am a teacher. |
| Stal se **lékařem**. | He became a doctor. |
